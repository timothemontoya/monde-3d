# Rendus d'inspiration avec Nano Banana

Pour chaque zone : une **photo de base** prise dans le jeu (`NN-zone.jpg`, dans ce dossier) et un
**prompt** pour que Nano Banana la transforme en illustration de concept art. Le but n'est pas de
copier le résultat, mais d'y piocher des idées : formes des bâtiments, palette, détails au sol,
ambiance, éléments de décor à ajouter.

## Mode d'emploi

1. Dans Gemini (Nano Banana), joins la photo de la zone.
2. Colle le **style commun** puis le **prompt de la zone** (en un seul message).
3. Itère en demandant des variantes ciblées, par exemple :
   - `Same image, but at night with lanterns and moonlight.`
   - `Same image, closer to the ground, from the player's eye level.`
   - `Keep everything, but make the houses taller and narrower.`
   - `Show only the central building in more detail.`
4. Pour une idée sans contrainte de composition, retire la phrase « Keep the camera angle… ».

Les prompts sont en anglais : les modèles d'image suivent mieux les consignes dans cette langue.

## Style commun (à mettre au début de chaque prompt)

```
Transform this screenshot of my low-poly 3D exploration game into a polished piece of concept art.
Keep the camera angle, the composition, the coastline and the position of the main buildings,
walls and paths, so the result can guide how I build this place in the game.
Style: stylized low-poly fantasy with clean, chunky shapes, a warm fairy-tale mood that is slightly
melancholic, painterly lighting, soft volumetric light rays, atmospheric depth fog, rich but
harmonious colors, lots of small ground details (grass tufts, flowers, rocks, fences, props).
No text, no UI, no characters in the foreground.
```

## 01 · Le Village blanc — `01-village-blanc.jpg`

```
This is the starting village where the player wakes up without knowing why. A peaceful walled
village: cream stone ramparts with square towers and a single gate, from which a dirt road leaves
toward the open lands. Inside, two rings of half-timbered houses with terracotta roofs face a round
market square with a stone fountain, a campfire, market stalls and a wooden cart. Golden autumn trees
around the walls. Golden hour, warm beige mist, smoke from chimneys, lanterns along the main street,
small vegetable gardens and flower boxes between the houses. Make it feel lived-in and cozy.
```

## 02 · Les Terres libres — `02-terres-libres.jpg`

```
A no-man's land that touches every region of the continent: rolling golden meadows with groves of
golden autumn trees and open clearings full of wildflowers. At the center, a campfire at a crossroads
where four dirt trails meet on a small packed-earth square, with a weathered wooden signpost, stones
and a lantern. In the distance: the walled white village, jagged mountains, and the edge of a dark
forest swallowed by a wall of violet magical mist. Soft afternoon light, wind in the grass,
abandoned things nobody claimed (an old cart, a broken fence, a mossy stone).
```

## 03 · La Cité volcanique — `03-cite-volcanique.jpg`

```
A city built in the shadow of an active volcano on a northern island. Dark ash ground, dead twisted
trees, basalt rocks. Dark stone houses with rust and charcoal roofs arranged in rings around a square,
with glowing orange windows and iron braziers at every corner. On the north side of the square, the
chieftain's manor: larger, with two pointed blood-red turrets. Behind, the volcano with a glowing
crater and a slow plume of grey smoke. Embers floating in the air, orange light from below, a reddish
hazy sky. Add forges, channels of lava, chains and banners in dark red.
```

## 04 · Les ruines de la Corne — `04-ruines-corne.jpg`

```
The ruins of an old village caught between two armies during the Battle of the Horn, between the
volcano city and the Atlanteans. Broken stone walls of houses arranged around a former square,
collapsed floors and burnt timbers, three broken columns, a destroyed catapult, a tattered war banner
on a pole, arrows and rusted shields half-buried in the ash. A wrecked ship lies on the shore below.
A dirt road still crosses the ruins. Grey and brown palette, cold overcast light, a few weeds and
small flowers growing back between the stones. A place of memory, melancholic and quiet.
```

## 05 · Le fort de l'Hippocampe — `05-fort-hippocampe.jpg`

```
A coastal outpost fort on a narrow peninsula, built so that the volcano city and the Atlanteans never
fight again: nobody crosses the strait in secret. Cream stone walls with four round watchtowers with
pointed roofs, a single gate facing the land, and in front of the sea a battery of black iron cannons
on the beach, aimed at the strait. Inside: tents, crates, barrels, a flag, a small campfire, guards'
equipment. A stone seahorse statue near the walls. Bright sea light, turquoise water, seagulls,
a sense of tension and vigilance.
```

## 06 · L'archipel des Atlantes — `06-archipel-atlantes.jpg`

```
An archipelago of three green islands linked by long suspended rope-and-plank bridges high above the
sea. Each island is named after one face of a three-faced deity: Oclène (abundance, the central island
with a harbor and a village of white houses with turquoise roofs along a street that winds down to the
port), Selenne (hope, a wooded transition island) and Eregone (luck, where the village master lives in
a larger manor with golden roofs and two pointed turrets). Add shrines or statues of the three-faced
deity, sails in the harbor, cliffs, waterfalls, golden-leaved trees. Clear, airy, Mediterranean light.
```

## 07 · Le village des sorcières — `07-village-sorcieres.jpg`

```
The village of the dark witches, deep in a forest where light struggles to enter. A circle of dark
wooden huts with deep purple roofs around a central fire, giant glowing violet mushrooms, bubbling
cauldrons, hanging bones and charms, lanterns with violet glass. Twisted trees with dark violet foliage
all around, and at the edge a curtain of thick magical violet mist. Nearby stands the queen's cabin,
taller, with one very tall pointed turret; its floor sounds hollow, as a portal sleeps beneath it.
Dusk light, purple fog, orange firelight, mysterious but beautiful.
```

## 08 · La forêt obscure — `08-foret-obscure.jpg`

```
The densest part of the witches' forest: a crowded forest of twisted trees with dark violet leaves,
roots, moss, glowing mushrooms, thin shafts of light piercing the canopy, purple mist between the
trunks. A dirt path enters the forest through a powerful magical barrier: a wall of swirling violet
energy with a single glowing rune at its center, blocking the way. Make the barrier the focal point.
Mysterious, enchanted, a little threatening.
```

## 09 · Les Terres de glace — `09-terres-de-glace.jpg`

```
A white and silent land of snowy hills. Groves of frost-covered blue-green pine trees, some heavy with
snow, scattered bluish rocks, a lonely campfire in a clearing where a dirt trail ends. Pale blue sky,
icy mist, sparkling snow, soft cold light. In the distance, a sheer ice crest with a single narrow
pass, closed by a wall of thick frost. Add frozen ponds, icicles on rocks, footprints in the snow,
an old abandoned sled.
```

## 10 · Le Royaume de glace — `10-royaume-de-glace.jpg`

```
Behind ramparts of ice sits only the ice king and his ghosts. Pale blue ice walls with square towers
enclose a snowy plateau; in the middle rises the frost palace: a tall central keep with a pointed roof
and four smaller towers, all made of translucent glowing ice, with icy banners. Giant ice spikes around
it, a frozen sea at its feet, towering ice cliffs behind. Add faint ghostly figures on the walls,
cold blue light coming from inside the palace, aurora borealis in the sky. Majestic and eerie.
```

## 11 · La Cité des montagnes et du vent — `11-cite-du-vent.jpg`

```
A city clinging to the slopes of wind-battered mountains, where the wind decides. Houses with stone
ground floors and wooden upper floors, ochre roofs, arranged in rings broken by two streets. Make the

flapping on poles, wind chimes, ropes and pulleys, bent grass, kites. Jagged rocky peaks around,
bright sky with fast-moving clouds, dry golden light.
```

```
A city that nobody returns from, hidden inside a perfect ring of steep grey mountains forming a natural

Invent the city inside the ring: pale stone architecture with strange organic shapes, towers that

Keep the ring of mountains from the screenshot.
```

# Rendus d'inspiration avec Nano Banana

Pour chaque zone : joins la photo `NN-zone.jpg` (dans ce dossier) dans Gemini, puis colle **le bloc
entier** de la zone. Chaque bloc contient déjà le style commun : un seul copier-coller.

Pour itérer ensuite : `Same image, but at night.` · `Same image, from the player's eye level.` ·
`Show only the main building, in more detail.`

## 01 · Le Village blanc

```
Transform this screenshot of my low-poly 3D exploration game into a polished piece of concept art. Keep the camera angle, the composition, the coastline and the position of the main buildings, walls and paths, so the result can guide how I build this place in the game. Style: stylized low-poly fantasy with clean, chunky shapes, a warm fairy-tale mood that is slightly melancholic, painterly lighting, soft volumetric light rays, atmospheric depth fog, rich but harmonious colors, lots of small ground details (grass tufts, flowers, rocks, fences, props). No text, no UI, no characters in the foreground.

This is the starting village where the player wakes up without knowing why. A peaceful walled village on a cliff above the sea: cream stone ramparts with square towers, a main gate where a dirt road leaves toward the open lands, and a second small gate toward a fishing cove. Inside, rings of cream half-timbered houses with dark red-brown roofs face a round packed-earth market square with a large stone fountain, a campfire, market stalls, benches and a cart. Vegetable gardens between the houses, chimney smoke, lanterns along a circular street. Golden autumn groves outside the walls and a meadow full of white and colored flowers in front of the gate. Golden hour, warm mist. Make it feel lived-in and cozy.
```
